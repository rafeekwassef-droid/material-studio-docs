Material Studio is undergoing testing and is not yet available for sale. This guide describes the current theme. Complete a test purchase and review every page before publishing your store.

Install and set up

  1. In Shopify admin, open Online Store → Themes → Import theme → Upload zip file. Select the theme ZIP. Keep it as a draft while setting up.
  2. Choose Edit theme. Under Theme settings, select your fonts, colors, logo, favicon, product-card layout, and social links.
  3. Set both the background and matching text color for accent, dark, and reference surfaces. Check button text and links after each color change.
  4. In Header, choose your main menu and customer account menu. In Footer, choose your footer menu, newsletter content, and country/language selector visibility.
  5. Add your products and collections in Shopify admin, then connect them using the section resource pickers.
  6. Preview on a phone and desktop. Check links, product options, the cart, delivery information, contact details, and policies before publishing.

Images and catalog content belong to your store. Installing the theme does not import the demonstration products or photography.

Build your homepage

Use Add section in the editor to insert a section. Drag its handle to reorder it. Use the section menu to duplicate or remove it, or the eye icon to hide it. Save your changes, and check the mobile preview.

Material hero

Write a concise heading and introduction. Add material blocks with an image, material name, and collection or page link. Choose a fallback palette for blocks without an image. Set each image focal point in Shopify so important details remain visible on small screens.

Room study

Select a room photograph, describe the space, and add product blocks using the product picker. Each block can include a short detail and link label. Link the section to a page with the full room story. Products remain accessible without clicking image hotspots.

Material to object

Select a material image and describe its actual finish. Add process blocks for steps you can substantiate, with an image, heading, and short explanation. Link to your material guide or collection.

Selected products

Choose the collection you want to feature, set the product limit, and review your global product-card settings. Choose a populated collection whose products are available to the Online Store channel.

Sample products

Choose a collection containing your normal Shopify sample products. Add an optional trade message and destination link. The theme does not create sample orders separately or change your fulfillment rules.

Projects and journal

Add story blocks with an image, heading, summary, and link to a Shopify page or blog article. Write meaningful image alt text in your media library.

Trade callout

Explain your actual trade service and link to your trade information or contact page. Replace the example copy with your own eligibility and service details.

Products and samples

Add descriptions, media, price, inventory, SKU, and product options in Shopify admin. Assign a template in the product's Theme template field. The sample template provides a sample-focused information layout.

In the product template, retain the variant picker and buy buttons for products with options. The picker asks Shopify for the selected combination; unavailable combinations cannot be added to the cart. Quantity and app input values are preserved when options change.

Add specification-summary blocks for material, finish, dimensions, and compatibility. Enter accurate values or connect compatible dynamic sources with the editor's dynamic-source icon. Blank optional values are omitted. No particular metafield namespace is required. Template text is shared by products using that template; use dynamic sources or separate templates when values differ.

Add detail blocks for care, installation, shipping, and downloads. Use the sample-link block to select a relevant sample product. Do not place purchase-critical restrictions only inside collapsed content.

Upload images, Shopify-hosted video, supported external video, or 3D models to the product's media list. Check the media on mobile as well as desktop. Product media and variant media are managed in Shopify, not by uploading files to theme code.

Selling plans require a configured subscription app or Shopify selling-plan integration. Keep the purchase-options block when using subscriptions. Pickup availability depends on location inventory and pickup configuration. Payment terms and accelerated checkout depend on store and payment eligibility. The theme cannot enable these services by itself.

For app content, install and configure the app, then use Add block → Apps where supported. Use Custom Liquid for trusted custom markup and review its accessibility and loading cost. Third-party app behavior needs its own testing.

Navigation and discovery

Create menus in Shopify admin and select them in Header and Footer. Use clear labels and test nested menus with both keyboard and touch. Enable customer accounts in Shopify settings, then choose the customer account menu in Header.

Organize normal Shopify collections by category, material, or room. Configure available filters using Shopify Search & Discovery. Filters are supplied by Shopify; they will only appear when configured and applicable to the results. Test a filter with zero matches and clear it to confirm the full collection returns.

Predictive search presents suggestions as you type. Use the arrow keys to move through suggestions, Enter to open one, and Escape to close them. Submitting the search form opens the full results page.

Create your contact page in Shopify and assign its contact template. Storefront customer messages use Shopify's store contact delivery settings. The theme-support form on this website is for merchants seeking help with Material Studio.

Languages and accessibility

Use Shopify's theme content editor for interface wording, and a compatible translation workflow for published languages and merchant content. Theme settings and section text must also be translated. Country choices depend on Markets configuration; language choices depend on published store languages.

The page direction follows the selected locale, including Arabic and Hebrew. Check your own long translations, media crops, menus, and app widgets in every language you publish.

Use descriptive links and image alt text, a clear main heading, and strong text/background contrast. Avoid putting essential text inside images. Review the storefront at 200% zoom, with keyboard navigation, and with reduced motion enabled after changing settings or installing apps.

Updates and troubleshooting

How should I update?

Duplicate the installed theme first. Keep a record of Custom Liquid and code changes. Install the update as a draft, check settings and app integrations, test product options and the cart, then publish when satisfied. Keep the prior theme as a rollback copy.

Why is a product or section empty?

Check the selected resource, collection membership, publication to the Online Store channel, and market availability. Select an image where needed. Some empty-state guidance is visible only inside the editor.

Why can a product not be added?

Check the selected variant, inventory, purchase options, and any required recipient fields. If an option update fails, choose the option again or reload. Purchase stays disabled during a failed update to prevent ordering the wrong variant.

Why is my template not listed?

Template assignment reflects the published theme. Keep the matching template in your published theme when assigning it, or preview the draft template from the editor while testing.

What should I include in a support request?

Send your store URL, theme version, affected page, browser/device, reproduction steps, and an optional screenshot. Never send passwords, payment details, or customer information. Use the merchant support form.
